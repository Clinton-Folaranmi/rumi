Rules

The few things every screen follows. When a new piece is made, it should pass all of these.

1
Say less; details on hover.

Labels are a word or two. Anything longer goes in a hint (data-tip) that appears instantly, with no delay. Lists show a thing's name; its path is in the hint. A hint never covers what you're choosing between: in menus it sits to the side.

2
Area shows size, and only what can carry a name is drawn.

A cartridge's size is its area. Its label is a name and, when it fits, one size chip. Anything too small to name folds into one "N more" tray. No specks, no slivers.

3
Every mark means one thing.

Colour is the kind of content; a folder takes the colour of most of what's inside. A tab in the outline means folder: it opens. The stripe on a folder shows the mix inside. An icon is an app. A white outlined area is an opened folder. A tray of spines is the small leftovers. Graphite is macOS, locked. A dashed slot is something gone. Pink (--accent) is what you've picked and the one main button. In the Changed view only, colour says which way a thing went: orange grew, green shrank. Nothing is decoration.

4
Filters bring things forward, never fade them.

A filter changes what the map shows. Never dim the rest as a way to filter.

5
Every control shows its state.

Rest, hover, open (its menu or panel is showing), on (applied). Open and on can be true at once and must look different from each other.

6
Motion is quick and never in the way.

Only transform and opacity move. 120–320 ms, easing out. Input is never blocked: a click mid-motion finishes the motion and acts.

Cartridges

Everything on the map is a cartridge: a plastic shell in its kind's colour with a printed label. A folder has a tab and a stripe. The label is a name and a size chip; the tier depends on the room it has.

What changed

Every scan is compared with an earlier one. A chip under the big number says by how much the disk moved; clicking it swaps the map for only what changed, sized by how much.

Two colours: orange grew or is new, green shrank (here, and only here, colour isn't the kind). Removed is an empty dashed slot, like free space on the shelf. One number per cartridge: the change, signed. The label's heading says which way (Grew, New, Shrank, Removed). Hint on the chip: the three things that moved most. The comparison scan stays the same for the day: it's replaced when the scan being retired is 12 hours older than the new one.

List rows

The lists on the right. A row is: what it is (tile) · name and why · size. The name is the thing's own name, never its path: the path is in the hint. Where a name can't tell rows apart (copies of one file), the folder that differs follows, faint. No checkbox: clicking anywhere on the row picks it. A picked row's cartridge icon shows a pink tick in its label and its size turns pink; nothing else changes. The Trash is a row like any other ("Can't undo"); the black tray is the only place that acts.

Search

One search, two doors: ⌘F in the app, and the cartridge in the menu bar (or ⌃⌥Space from any app), which opens the same panel over whatever you're doing. Rumi stays in the menu bar when its window is closed, and can start at login from the cartridge's menu.
